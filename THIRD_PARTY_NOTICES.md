## MuScriptor model weights — CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial)

The MuScriptor checkpoints were released by Kyutai and Mirelo under the
Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) and are redistributed as
GGUF files at https://huggingface.co/DamRsn/muscriptor-gguf under the same

**They may only be used for non-commercial purposes.** The Apache-2.0 licence
covers NeuralSheet's code only, not the weights. Paper:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08168 · Project: https://github.com/muscriptor/muscriptor

## HTDemucs model weights — MIT

Stem separation uses the four-source Hybrid Transformer Demucs checkpoint
(`ggml-model-htdemucs-4s-f16.bin`), which NeuralSheet downloads on demand into
`~/Library/NeuralSheet/models` from the `Retrobear/demucs.cpp` dataset on Hugging
Face (https://huggingface.co/datasets/Retrobear/demucs.cpp, pinned to a commit in
`app/Packages/NeuralSheetCore/Sources/NeuralSheetCore/ModelManifest.swift`). Those
files are MIT-licensed conversions of Meta's Demucs weights; Demucs itself is MIT,
Copyright (c) Meta Platforms, Inc. and affiliates
(https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs). **No weights are included in this
repository or in the app.**

## Engine test audio fixture — CC BY 4.0

`app/Packages/NeuralSheetEngine/Tests/NeuralSheetEngineTests/Fixtures/audio/fixture_3chunks_16k.wav`
is a fifteen-second excerpt of "You Drive Me Insane" by Jon Worthy and the Bends,
from the album *Only A Dream*
(https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Jon_Worthy_and_the_Bends/Only_A_Dream/You_Drive_Me_Insane/),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It was downmixed to mono and
resampled to 16 kHz; the attribution and the changes are recorded in the README
beside it. It is used by the engine's tests and is not part of the shipped app.
